How to use
  • Step through the video and press M (or the green button) at every bottom dead center (lowest point of a bounce). C marks a cut in the video (a change of shot).
  • Click or drag on the timelines to jump. Drag a green mark to move it, Del removes the mark nearest to the play position.
  • Your marks are kept in this browser automatically. Use Save my marks to get a video-XX.bdc.json file and put it next to the video in the repository's videos/ folder.
  • With Body pose the analysis also tracks head, shoulders, elbows, wrists, hips, knees and feet (the first run loads about 20 MB). The skeleton is drawn on the video; the chart below the timeline shows the knee angle, body tilt and feet height. Automatic points are then moved to the lowest point of the feet.
  • Type the name of the trick into Trick of this bounce while the video is inside that bounce. The names are saved with your marks.
  • Tick Hide automatic points if you want to mark without seeing the computer's answer first.
